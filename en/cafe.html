Specialty coffee, bistro spirit

For coffee lovers, Franquette serves specialty coffee, roasted by Ïota in Gordes, in the Luberon. And for everyone else: matcha, mint tea, juice of the day…

Cookies, cakes and soft-boiled eggs, from breakfast to afternoon snack. And at lunchtime, our ravioles: Franquette’s signature dish since 2019!
